Common Questions

Before you
book a call.

Is Spa JVC an actual spa?

No — think of us as a local concierge, not a spa. We're a separate, independent matching layer. We connect you with vetted, independently-run spas in JVC; treatments happen at their premises.

Does it cost anything to use?

Nothing. The only payment involved is the spa's usual treatment fee, paid on the day — connecting you with them costs nothing.

How fast will I hear back?

During the day, a reply from your matched spa typically lands within a quarter of an hour.

Are the spas licensed?

Every spa in our JVC network is checked for a valid trade licence and hygiene standards before we add them.
